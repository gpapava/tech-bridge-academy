# Tech Bridge Academy — Demo Script

## Before You Start

Open **http://localhost:3000** in your browser. Make sure you are **not logged in** so the audience sees the public view first.

---

## Part 1 — Introduction (2 minutes)

**Say this:**

> "Good morning / afternoon everyone. Today I want to show you the Tech Bridge Academy platform. This is the digital tool we built as part of our Erasmus+ project — project number 2024-1-IT01-KA220-VET-000255000.
>
> The idea is simple. Schools and companies in the mechanical engineering sector need to work together more. But it is hard to find the right partner, share good practices, or co-design training programmes. This platform helps them do exactly that — in one place, online, across Europe.
>
> Let me show you how it works."

---

## Part 2 — The Home Page

**Action:** Point to the top of the screen.

> "This is the home page. At the top you can see our logo and the Erasmus+ project reference. The platform has three main areas: The Bridge, SME Services, and School Services. We will look at each one.
>
> You can also see some live numbers here — how many schools are registered, how many companies, how many matches the system has made, and how many good practices are published."

---

## Part 3 — Public Pages

### About Page
**Action:** Click **About** in the menu.

> "The About page explains what the project is, who it is for, and what problems it solves. This is useful for organisations who are new to the platform."

### Partners Page
**Action:** Click **Partners** in the menu.

> "Here you can see all the partner organisations in our consortium. Each partner has a profile with their country, role, and contact information."

### News & Events
**Action:** Click **News**, then go back and click **Events**.

> "The platform has a news section for project updates, and an events calendar. Partners can post webinars, workshops, or meetings here so everyone stays informed."

---

## Part 4 — The Bridge

**Action:** Click **Bridge** in the menu to open the dropdown.

> "The Bridge is the core of the platform. It has five services."

### Organisation Directory
**Action:** Click **Organisation Directory**.

> "This is the directory. You can see all registered schools and companies. You can filter by country, type, or sector. Click on any organisation to see their full profile — what they do, what they are looking for, their location, and their contact details."

**Action:** Click on one organisation profile.

> "Each profile shows all the important information. Schools can see which companies work in their area of interest. Companies can see which schools offer the training they need."

### Repository of Practices
**Action:** Go back and click **Repository of Practices**.

> "This is the repository. Here, schools and companies share real examples of good collaboration — internship programmes, co-designed courses, joint projects. Anyone can browse these. Members can also submit their own."

**Action:** Click on one practice to open it.

> "Each entry has a description, the country, the type of collaboration, and who submitted it. It is a knowledge bank for the whole network."

### Career Guidance
**Action:** Go back and click **Career Guidance**.

> "This section has resources for career guidance — tools and content that schools can use with their students to help them understand the job market in mechanical engineering."

### Co-Design Service
**Action:** Go back and click **Co-Design Service**.

> "The co-design service is for schools and companies who want to work together on curriculum design. They can connect here and start a structured conversation about what skills students need and how training programmes can be improved."

### Educational Collaboration
**Action:** Go back and click **Educational Collaboration**.

> "This section supports broader educational collaboration — things like teacher exchanges, joint research, and shared training modules between schools across different countries."

---

## Part 5 — Registration

**Action:** Click **Register Free** in the top right corner.

> "Registration is free. An organisation can sign up as a school or as a company. They fill in their basic information and create an account. Once registered, their profile goes through a short validation step by the admin team — this keeps the directory trustworthy."

---

## Part 6 — Member Area (logged in view)

**Action:** Go to **/auth/login** and log in with the school demo account:
- Email: `info@iis-volta.edu.it`
- Password: `School@123`

> "Now I will log in as a school to show you the member area."

### Dashboard
**Action:** You will land on the Dashboard automatically.

> "This is the dashboard. The member can see their organisation profile, their match suggestions, any active surveys, and their notifications — all in one place.
>
> The match suggestions are important. The system automatically compares the profile of this school with all the companies in the platform and gives a match score. The higher the score, the better the fit. This saves a lot of time — the school does not have to search manually."

### School Services
**Action:** Click **Schools** in the top menu (visible because you are logged in as a school).

> "Logged-in schools have access to extra services. They can connect with other schools in the network, find staff development resources, access the curriculum observatory, and plan company visits."

**Action:** Log out, then log in with the company account:
- Email: `hr@precisionfab.it`
- Password: `Company@123`

### SME Services
**Action:** Click **SMEs** in the top menu.

> "Companies have their own area too. They can browse the SME network, access HR coaching resources, and fill in the annual skills needs survey. The survey results help us understand what skills are missing in the sector — this is very useful data for schools when they update their programmes."

## Part 8 — Closing (1 minute)

> "So, to summarise — Tech Bridge Academy gives schools and companies a structured way to find each other, share knowledge, and build better training programmes together. It is free to use, it is funded by the European Union through Erasmus+, and it is available to all partner organisations and their networks.
>
> We would love for all of you to register your organisation and start using it. If you have questions, I am happy to answer them now."
