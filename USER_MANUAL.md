# Tech Bridge Academy — User Manual

**Platform URL:** https://tech-bridge-academy.vercel.app
**Project:** Erasmus+ KA220-VET · No. 2024-1-IT01-KA220-VET-000255000

---

## What Is Tech Bridge Academy?

Tech Bridge Academy is a digital platform that connects VET schools and manufacturing companies. It helps them find each other, share good practices, and work together on training and education.

The platform is free to use for all partner organisations and their networks.

---

## 1. Creating an Account

1. Go to **https://tech-bridge-academy.vercel.app**
2. Click **Register Free** in the top right corner
3. Choose your account type:
   - **School / VET Institution** — for schools, colleges, and training centres
   - **Company / SME** — for businesses and manufacturing companies
4. Fill in your name, email address, and a password
5. Accept the privacy policy and click **Create Account**
6. You will be taken to your Dashboard

> **Note:** Registration is free. No payment is required.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Click **Log in** in the top right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. You will be taken to your Dashboard

---

## 3. Creating Your Organisation Profile

After registering, you need to create a profile for your organisation. This is important — without a profile you will not appear in the directory and you will not receive match suggestions.

1. From the Dashboard, click **Create Organisation Profile**
2. Fill in all the fields:
   - **Organisation name**
   - **Description** — what your organisation does
   - **Mission** — your goals and values
   - **Sectors** — the industries you work in (e.g. mechanical engineering, manufacturing)
   - **Regions** — where you are located
   - **Needs** — what you are looking for from a partner
   - **Opportunities** — what you can offer to a partner
   - **Tags** — keywords that describe your organisation
   - **Contact details** — email, phone, website
3. Click **Save Profile**

Your profile will be sent for review. Once approved by the admin team, it will appear in the directory and you will start receiving match suggestions.

> **Tip:** The more detail you add to your profile, the better your match suggestions will be.

## 4. Browsing the Platform (No Login Required)

Some parts of the platform are open to everyone, even without an account.

### Home Page
The home page shows a summary of the platform — number of registered schools, companies, matches, and good practices.

### About
Explains the TECH BRIDGE VET project, its goals, and the partner countries involved.

### Partners
Shows all the organisations in the Erasmus+ consortium with their details.

### News
Project updates, articles, and announcements from the consortium.

### Events
A calendar of webinars, workshops, and meetings. You can see dates, locations, and whether the event is online or in person.

## 5. The Bridge

The Bridge is the main area of the platform. It has five services.

### Organisation Directory
- Click **Bridge → Organisation Directory** in the menu
- Browse all registered schools and companies
- Use the filters to search by country, type, or sector
- Click on any organisation to see their full profile

### Repository of Good Practices
- Click **Bridge → Repository of Practices**
- Browse real examples of school-company collaboration from across Europe
- Each entry shows a description, country, sector, and attached documents
- If you are a member, you can submit your own practice by clicking **Submit a Practice**

### Career Guidance
- Click **Bridge → Career Guidance**
- Find resources and tools to help students understand career opportunities in mechanical engineering
- Content is free to use and share

### Co-Design Service
- Click **Bridge → Co-Design Service**
- This service helps schools and companies work together on curriculum design
- You can submit a co-design request to start a structured collaboration with a partner

### Educational Collaboration
- Click **Bridge → Educational Collaboration**
- Find resources for teacher development, joint research, and shared training modules between schools

## 6. Your Dashboard

After logging in, you will land on your Dashboard. This is your personal area.

**What you can see on the Dashboard:**

- **My Organisation Profile** — your profile and its current status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected)
- **Match Suggestions** — organisations that the platform has identified as good partners for you, ranked by match score
- **Active Surveys** — surveys you have been invited to complete
- **Notifications** — messages from the admin team
- **Quick Actions** — shortcuts to the most useful pages

---

## 7. Understanding Match Suggestions

The platform automatically compares your profile with all other organisations and calculates a match score out of 100.

A higher score means a better fit. The score is based on:
- Whether you are a school and the other organisation is a company (or vice versa)
- Whether you work in the same sectors
- Whether you are in the same region
- Whether you share similar interests and keywords
- Whether your needs match the other organisation's opportunities

You do not need to do anything to get matches — they are calculated automatically by the admin team when they run the matching engine.

To view a match, click **View →** next to any suggestion. You will see the other organisation's full profile.

## 8. For Schools — School Services

If you are registered as a school, you have access to extra services under **Schools** in the menu.

- **School Networking** — find and connect with other schools in the network
- **Staff Development** — resources for teacher training and professional development
- **Curriculum Observatory** — tools and information to help update your programmes based on industry needs
- **Company Visits** — resources for planning visits to partner companies

---

## 9. For Companies — SME Services

If you are registered as a company, you have access to extra services under **SMEs** in the menu.

- **SME Networking** — browse and connect with other companies in the network
- **HR Coaching** — resources to help with recruitment, training, and workforce development
- **Skills Needs Survey** — fill in the annual survey to report what skills are missing in your sector. This data helps schools update their training programmes.

---

## 10. Editing Your Profile

1. Go to your Dashboard
2. Click **Edit My Profile**
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Save**

Your updated profile will be visible immediately.

---

## 11. Profile Status

After you create or update your profile, it goes through a short review:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Your profile is waiting for review by the admin team (usually 1–2 business days) |
| **Approved** | Your profile is live in the directory and you are receiving match suggestions |
| **Rejected** | Your profile needs changes — check your notifications for details |
