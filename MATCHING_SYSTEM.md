# Tech Bridge Academy — Matching System

## Overview

The matching engine automatically scores every pair of approved school and company profiles on a scale of **0 to 100**. Pairs that score 30 or above are saved as match suggestions and sent to admins for review. Once approved by an admin, they appear on each organisation's dashboard.

The engine runs in two ways:
- **Automatically** — whenever a new profile is approved
- **Manually** — when an admin clicks "Run Matching Engine" on the admin dashboard

---

## How the Score is Calculated

The score is built from five dimensions. Each contributes a maximum number of points:

| Dimension | Max Points | What it measures |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Complementary type | 20 | Is one a school and the other a company? |
| Sector overlap | 25 | How many sectors do they share? |
| Region overlap | 20 | How many regions do they share? |
| Tag overlap | 15 | How many keywords/tags do they share? |
| Needs ↔ Opportunities text | 20 | Do the school's needs match the company's offer, or vice versa? |
| **Total** | **100** | |

---

### 1. Complementary Type — 20 pts

A school matched with a company gets the full 20 points automatically. Two schools or two companies matched together get 0.

This reflects the core purpose of the platform: connecting education with industry.

---

### 2. Sector Overlap — up to 25 pts

Uses **Jaccard similarity** — a standard formula for comparing two sets:

```
Jaccard = (items in common) / (total unique items across both)
```

**Example:**
- School sectors: `CNC Machining, Welding, Robotics`
- Company sectors: `CNC Machining, Welding, Hydraulics`
- In common: `CNC Machining, Welding` = 2
- Total unique: `CNC Machining, Welding, Robotics, Hydraulics` = 4
- Jaccard = 2/4 = 0.5 → **12 pts** (50% of 25)

The more sectors they share relative to all sectors listed, the higher the score.

---

### 3. Region Overlap — up to 20 pts

Same Jaccard formula applied to regions. Two organisations operating in the same geographic area score higher than two from completely different regions.

**Example:**
- School: `Lombardy, Piedmont`
- Company: `Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna`
- In common: `Lombardy` = 1
- Total unique: 3
- Jaccard = 1/3 = 0.33 → **7 pts**

---

### 4. Tag Overlap — up to 15 pts

Same Jaccard formula applied to keywords/tags. Tags are free-form descriptors like `apprenticeship`, `CNC`, `WBL`, `Industry 4.0`, `robotics`.

The more specific and overlapping the tags, the higher the score. This rewards profiles that are detailed and precise.

---

### 5. Needs ↔ Opportunities Text Match — up to 20 pts

This is the most intelligent dimension. It compares the free-text fields:

- Does **what the school says it needs** match **what the company says it offers**?
- Does **what the company says it needs** match **what the school says it offers**?

The engine tokenizes both texts (splits into individual keywords, removes common words like "with", "that", "from"), then runs Jaccard similarity on the resulting word sets.

**Example:**
- Company needs: *"graduates skilled in CNC programming, quality control, and 3D printing"*
- School offers: *"training in CNC machining, dimensional quality inspection, and additive manufacturing"*

The words `cnc`, `quality`, `training` overlap → strong signal → up to 20 pts

The engine takes the stronger of the two directions (school→company or company→school).

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 80–100 | Excellent Match | Strong alignment across all dimensions |
| 60–79 | Good Match | Solid overlap, high collaboration potential |
| 40–59 | Moderate Match | Some alignment, worth exploring |
| 30–39 | Weak Match | Minimal overlap, included for admin review |
| 0–29 | Not saved | Below threshold, discarded |

---

## Co-Design Flag

If a match scores **60 or above** and involves a school and a company with strong sector alignment (12+ sector points), the engine adds a special flag:

> ⚡ High potential for curriculum co-design collaboration

This appears in the match reasons and signals that the two organisations could jointly design a training module or curriculum pathway.

---

## Match Reports

For matches scoring **50 or above**, the engine automatically generates a written match report. It includes:

- Executive summary of why it's a good match
- Shared sectors and regions
- Specific collaboration suggestions (internships, guest lectures, co-design, challenge-based projects)
- Recommended next steps

If an AI API key is configured (`OPENAI_API_KEY` or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in `.env`), the report is generated by GPT-4o-mini or Claude Haiku. Otherwise it is generated by the rule-based engine using the scoring data.

---

## Admin Workflow

1. Profiles are submitted by organisations
2. Admin approves profiles → matching engine runs automatically
3. Admin reviews match suggestions at `/admin/matches`
4. Admin can **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Send Report** for each match
5. Approved matches appear on both organisations' dashboards with the score and reasons

---

## How to Improve Your Score

**For schools:**
- Select all relevant sectors precisely — avoid over-selecting unrelated ones
- Fill in regions where you actually operate or want to collaborate
- Add detailed tags (e.g. `CNC`, `WBL`, `apprenticeship`, `mechanical engineering`)
- Write detailed "What We Need" text — be specific about the skills, placements, or industry input you're looking for
- Write detailed "What We Offer" text — describe your facilities, curriculum areas, student profiles

**For companies:**
- Same applies — the more specific your needs and offers, the better the text cross-match
- A company that says "we need graduates with CNC programming skills and quality control experience" will match far better than one that says "we need skilled workers"

---

## Technical Notes

- The algorithm is implemented in `src/lib/matching/rule-based.ts`
- The orchestrator (saves matches to DB, generates reports, notifies admins) is in `src/lib/matching/index.ts`
- The AI report wrapper is in `src/lib/matching/ai-service.ts`
- Matching uses plain `fetch` to call AI APIs — no external SDKs required
- Match pairs are deduplicated: `(profileA, profileB)` and `(profileB, profileA)` are stored as one record with consistent ordering
- The threshold of 30 is configurable in `runFullMatching(threshold)` and `runBatchMatching(threshold)`
