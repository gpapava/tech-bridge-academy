Tensioner system for an industrial unwinder
Mechanical and automation 40–50 hours EQF adaptable Mechanical engineering · ITS / Technical institute
A company that designs and manufactures automatic machines for industrial lines wants to develop or improve the tensioner system of an unwinder. The unwinder accompanies the unwinding of large reels of wound material, maintaining the correct tension of the belt or strip during variation in the reel diameter.
Commission
Students are asked to analyse the functioning of the tensioner group, identify critical issues related to assembly, maintenance and reliability, and propose an improved technical solution. The Project Work reproduces the work of a small technical office: problem analysis, requirements definition, concept development, CAD modelling, technical documentation, and final presentation to the client.
Thematic areas
Mechanical design · Technical drawing and CAD · Mechanical technologies · Industrial automation · Maintenance · Quality and safety
Duration
Approximately 40–50 hours, adjustable based on the level of the programme and laboratory availability
Period
Second semester / second part of the training programme, preferably after acquiring basic competences in technical drawing, CAD and mechanical technology
Locations
Classroom · CAD laboratory · Mechanical laboratory · Optional company visit or meeting · Online sessions with the company tutor
Resources
Human: subject teacher, technical-practical teacher or lab instructor, company tutor, optional company technicians. Instrumental: PC, CAD software, measuring instruments, technical catalogues, company documentation, optional sample components
At the end of the pathway, students will be able to analyse a technical commission; interpret mechanical documentation and functional diagrams; identify design constraints; propose solutions consistent with the client's need; use CAD tools to represent components and assemblies; prepare drawings, bills of materials, and technical reports; collaborate in a group; document the work process; present and argue design choices to an external interlocutor.

The project develops in seven macro-phases, from the company presentation of the commission to the final evaluation. Duration is indicative and can be adjusted to the programme level.
1
Presentation of the commission by the company and initial context analysis
2
Study of the unwinder's functioning and the tensioner group
3
Identification of technical requirements and design constraints
4
Development of two or more solution hypotheses and motivated selection
5
CAD modelling of the assembly and principal components
6
Production of technical drawings, bill of materials, and brief technical report

Mid-point review with school and company tutors
