Embossing unit improvement for a tissue paper converting line
Mechanical and mechatronic 40–50 hours EQF adaptable Mechanics and mechatronics · ITS / Technical institute
A company specialising in the design and construction of automatic machines for tissue paper converting wants to improve the embossing unit installed on one of its production lines. Analysis of machine performance at client sites has revealed possible margins for improvement in embossing quality, group reliability, ease of maintenance, and efficiency of production operations.
Commission
Analyse the functioning of the embossing unit and develop an improvement proposal that is technically sustainable, taking into account functional, constructive, economic, and safety constraints. The solution must be technically plausible, documented, and presented to the company. The activity reproduces the work of an industrial design group.
Thematic areas
Design and improvement of products and processes · Mechanics and mechatronics · Technical drawing and CAD modelling · Production technologies · Quality, maintenance and safety · Work organisation and project management
Duration
Approximately 40–50 hours, adjustable based on the level of the programme and laboratory availability
Period
Second semester / second part of the training programme, preferably after acquiring basic competences in technical drawing, CAD and mechanical technology
Locations
Classroom · Technical laboratory · CAD laboratory · Company premises · Digital collaborative environments
Resources
Human: school tutor, company tutor, teachers, experts. Instrumental: laboratory, PC, CAD software, measuring instruments, technical documentation. Economic: ordinary school and company resources, optional consumable materials
At the end of the Project Work, students will be able to: interpret a commission from a company; analyse a complex mechanical system; identify improvement opportunities for a product or process; develop and compare design solutions; produce technical documentation consistent with the project; collaborate effectively in a work group; communicate their design choices professionally.

Joint school-company assessment. The evaluation considers: coherence of the proposal with the commission; technical quality of the design solution; capacity to analyse the problem; correctness of the documentation produced; capacity to work in a group; work organisation; capacity to present and argue design choices; critical reflection on the process followed.
